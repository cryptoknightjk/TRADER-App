Crypto TA Secrets TRADER App (web)
=================================

Open in Google Chrome or Brave:

  https://cryptoknightjk.github.io/TRADER-App/

No install. Your journal stays as a local CSV on your computer.

First use
---------
  1. Click Link Existing Journal
  2. Pick a folder, then pick your .csv
     (or Create New Journal)

  Mapping stays sticky until you click Change Location.

Optional starter file in this repo:
  journals/CK_Trading Journal Template_July 2026.csv
